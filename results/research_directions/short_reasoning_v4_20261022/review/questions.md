# Short reasoning questions for independent review

Forty synthetic two-choice questions. Select exactly one supported option, or flag the item if neither or both are supported.

## Q001

Option R charges a guaranteed fee of 3 credits and pays a gross prize of 15 credits with probability 2/5; otherwise it pays zero. Option S pays 4 credits with no fee. Choose the option with the larger expected net gain, subtracting any fee from the payout. Which should be chosen?

A. Option R

B. Option S

## Q002

A. 3/5

B. 3/4

## Q003

A. 70%

B. 66%

## Q004

## Q005

A. 20

B. 18
