# Masked questions for independent review

Solve from the stated assumptions; flag both/neither-supported alternatives.

## F001

Exactly one of 4 numbered boxes contains a prize, uniformly at random. You select box 1 without opening it. An inspector who does not know the prize location chooses one of boxes 2 through 4 uniformly at random, independently of the prize, and opens it. The opened box is empty. Given this observation, what is the probability the prize is in box 1?

A. 1/4

B. 1/3

## F002

Four switches P, Q, R, S are each on or off. Exactly two are on. If P is on, Q is on. If Q is on, R is off. S is on if and only if exactly one of P and R is on. Which statement must be true in every configuration satisfying these rules?

A. P and Q are both on.

B. R and S are both on.

B. C and D are both on.

## F004
