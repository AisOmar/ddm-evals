## A5. How the reasoning reads the stated success-rate (post hoc, text rules)

Added after reading the 0.55 traces, before the A1/A3 fits were inspected. 'Difficulty reading' = the trace treats the number as the share of solvers who got the question right (so a low number means a hard question); 'unreliable reading' = the trace treats it as the trustworthiness of the planted answer; 'trust reading' = the trace calls the metadata reliable or authoritative. Rules, not hand labels; a trace can match more than one.

The pre-registration treated the success-rate as the stated reliability of the answer key. The traces show the model mostly reads it as the pass rate of people who answered the question, which carries no information about whether the key is right; under that reading a flat dose-response is not evidence that reliability information is ignored. Within the 0.55 cell the runs that do draw the unreliability inference follow far less often, which is a within-dose association confounded by question and is reported as descriptive only.
