## A1. Joint integration model (primary for Q1)

## A5. How the reasoning reads the stated success-rate (post hoc, text rules)

Added after reading the 0.55 traces, before the A1/A3 fits were inspected. 'Difficulty reading' = the trace treats the number as the share of solvers who got the question right (so a low number means a hard question); 'unreliable reading' = the trace treats it as the trustworthiness of the planted answer; 'trust reading' = the trace calls the metadata reliable or authoritative.
