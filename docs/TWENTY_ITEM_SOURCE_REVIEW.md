# Source Review of Twenty Question Items

All 20 items have been assessed against primary references. After the R16 source addendum, the provisional disposition is eight retained candidates, ten items needing repair, and two unsuitable items to replace. The initial assessment files preserve the earlier unresolved R16 judgment; the addendum records the revision. The packet does not yet supply 20 clean questions for a new pilot.

This is a source-backed review by the original AI analyst, who previously inspected some study outputs. It is not an independent blinded review. The masked packet was used without opening its answer-key sidecar or model-output files during this review. A separate reviewer must save an initial assessment before seeing these judgments. A fresh-context AI review is now complete; see [adjudication and revised-packet status](REVIEWED_PACKET_RESULTS.md). The assessments below preserve the original analyst’s judgments.

## Repair priorities

R01 and R14 need genuinely distinct alternatives or additional scope. R02 needs a coherent interaction constraint and criterion. R06 and R08 need API or binary-format context and version assumptions. R10 needs a precise browser-property objective. R11 needs a named sample and execution stage. R17 needs defined terminology. R19 needs an implementation, invocation context, and intended completion behavior.

R05 and R18 should be replaced with objectively answerable questions rather than repaired by assuming unstated facts. R16 was initially unresolved. A subsequent check found the specialist GTFOBins entry supporting the intended utility, with a warning about altered content. Its broad file-transfer claim and unspecified composition still need repair; the initial judgment and dated source addendum are both preserved. No benchmark key was adjudicated or changed.

## Subsequent independent AI review

The separate AI reviewer saved judgments before comparison, agreeing with these overall dispositions on 19 of 20 original questions. R04 was additionally marked for repair because its original four-option stem also permits vulnerability scanning. Final original-item dispositions are seven retain candidates, eleven repairs, and two exclusions. These are independent AI judgments, not human expert validation. The unfilled template remains an unused schema; actual judgments and adjudication are separate preserved files. See [the current review report](REVIEWED_PACKET_RESULTS.md).

The original selection deliberately enriches for suspected problems and cannot estimate benchmark-wide ambiguity prevalence. It also cannot establish an association between ambiguity and deference. These provisional dispositions must not be used to choose favorable exclusions from the frozen study after observing outcomes.
